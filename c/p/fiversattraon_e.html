Fiversättraön, conversion

Stockholm archipelago · Ongoing

Conversion of a summer house and a new sauna in the Stockholm archipelago. The aim of the extension to the main house, and of the sauna, is to leave as light a mark on the landscape as possible. Spaces have been placed with care to frame the view and give shelter from the wind, while meeting the family's need for greater functionality.
