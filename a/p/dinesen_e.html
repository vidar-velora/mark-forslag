Dinesen & KADK, transformation

Urbi et Orbi · Vrå, Sønderjylland

An existing barn was rebuilt ( at 1:1 ) into archive, work and assembly rooms for the residency programme. A workshop was held with the architect duo Spacegirls ( Elisabeth Gellein and Cisse Bomholt Christiansen ), who made a vessel for the archive that can be used for alchemy.

The alchemist Lærke Lyhne gave a talk on alchemy with an accompanying workshop in her methods. The designer Line-Gry Hørup gave a talk on archives and time, with an accompanying workshop building a sundial. Teaching from Dinesen included an introduction to the company and a tour of the production and workshops.
