Ösmo, new building

Ösmo · Ongoing

A small dwelling added to a farmstead plot, taking its cue from the adjoining parstuga. The new building was intended to sit within the typology of the existing houses, to shelter from the wind, and to generate easily reached social spaces both indoors and out.
